## Field verdict
The bibliography is roughly **45 % macro/time-to-build + growth accounting**, **30 % national-accounts/measurement + intangibles**, **15 % demography**, **10 % wealth-accounting institutions**. Scholarly neighbourhood = **economic measurement / applied macro / national accounts**, with demography purely as method donor. It is *not* an innovation-economics or econometric-theory bibliography.

## Implication for journal targeting
Journals whose readership spans national-accounts measurement and applied growth/productivity are the natural fit; pure macro theory journals are not (the paper has no structural model); demographic journals are not.

# GATE 1.2 — Closest Prior Studies & Novelty Boundary

Selection: studies combining ≥2 of {capital formation timing, intangible assets, productivity/wealth measurement}, ranked by overlap.

## Closest studies (verified references)

**1. McGrattan & Prescott (2010), "Unmeasured Investment and the Puzzling US Boom in the 1990s", AEJ:Macro 2(4), 88–123. DOI 10.1257/mac.2.4.88.**
- Q: does unmeasured intangible investment explain the 1990s US TFP/productivity puzzle? Method: growth-model accounting, US only.
- Overlap: same core claim family — omitted investment inflates measured TFP. Strongest single overlap.
- Differentiation: they expand the *definition* of investment (quantum), not its *timing*; no gestation-lag parameter; single country; no wealth-side discipline.
- Remaining novelty: time-varying μ(t) channel + wealth-identity identification + 39-country coverage.

**2. Corrado, Hulten & Sichel (2009), "Intangible Capital and US Economic Growth", RIW 55(3), 661–685. DOI 10.1111/j.1475-4991.2009.00343.x.**
- Q: magnitude of intangible capital and its growth contribution (US). Overlap: β/intangible share concept; TFP re-statement.
- Differentiation: direct measurement of intangible categories vs our residual identification against a wealth identity; no timing channel.
- Remaining novelty: lag channel absent from CHS framework entirely.

**3. Corrado, Haskel, Iommi & Jona-Lasinio (2020), in Fraumeni (ed.), Measuring Economic Growth and Productivity, 363–385. DOI 10.1016/B978-0-12-817596-5.00016-0.**
- Cross-country intangible harmonisation (INTAN-Invest). Overlap: international intangible shares.
- Differentiation: they *build* the harmonised series; we bound the *residual gap* left when official accounts omit them.

**4. Koeva (2000), "The Facts About Time-to-Build", IMF WP 00/138.**
- Empirical lag distributions across project types. Overlap: gestation-lag magnitudes feeding M_obs weights.
- Differentiation: time-invariant lags; single-economy focus; no productivity-accounting consequence drawn.

**5. Kydland & Prescott (1982), Econometrica 50(6), 1345–1370. DOI 10.2307/1913386.**
- Origin of time-to-build in aggregate models. Overlap: lag kernel concept.
- Differentiation: calibration device for business cycles, not measurement; fixed lag.

**6. Edge (2007), "Time-to-build, time-to-plan, habit-persistence, and the liquidity effect", JME 54(6), 1644–1669. DOI 10.1016/j.jmoneco.2006.07.003.**
- Estimates lag structures in a monetary model. Closest *estimated* (not calibrated) lag work.
- Differentiation: US, DSGE context, time-invariant; no capital-measurement implication.

**7. Lange et al. (2021), The Changing Wealth of Nations 2021, World Bank. DOI 10.1596/978-1-4648-1590-4.**
- CWON produced-capital accounts = our wealth-side benchmark. Overlap: object of measurement.
- Differentiation: CWON is a statistical compilation, not a model of its own flow–stock inconsistency.

**8. Feenstra, Inklaar & Timmer (2015), AER 105(10), 3150–3182. DOI 10.1257/aer.20130954.**
- PWT construction incl. capital/depreciation methodology. Overlap: the measurement conventions we perturb.
- Differentiation: PWT documents conventions; we quantify their timing/coverage bias.

**9. Arrow et al. (2012), "Sustainability and the measurement of wealth", EDE 17(3), 317–353. DOI 10.1017/S1355770X12000137.**
- Wealth-accounting theory (IWI lineage). Overlap: wealth-vs-flow consistency motivation.
- Differentiation: sustainability focus; no gestation/intangible decomposition of the flow–stock gap.

**10. Bongaarts & Feeney (1998), PDR 24(2), 271–291. DOI 10.2307/2807974.**
- Tempo–quantum decomposition (fertility). Overlap: method donor — the mathematical transplant.
- Differentiation: entirely different literature; our contribution is the transplant + its empirical content.

## Novelty boundary (calibrated statement)
What this manuscript establishes that prior work does not:
1. A **time-varying** aggregate investment-to-output lag μ(t) estimated for 39 economies (prior work: fixed or regime-switching lags, mostly single-country).
2. A country-specific intangible share β identified by **jointly** disciplining production residuals and an independent wealth-account trajectory (prior work: direct category measurement or pure-model accounting).
3. A parameter-free observable tempo proxy M_obs from existing OECD asset detail.
4. A Brass relational-model diagnostic (RPIM) for PIM-vs-wealth consistency — no prior application found.
Claims bounded to "to our knowledge"; no "first ever".

## Positioning assessment
- **A. Measurement-first** — evidence support: strong (K gap, TFP/LS shifts are measurement re-classifications); novelty: clear; reviewer objection: "magnitudes modest (~4 %)" — honest answer: systematic + concentrated, and point is that residual absorbs it. **Recommended.**
- **B. Applied growth accounting** — support: partial (Wilcoxon n.s., β 7/39); risk: reads as weak growth-accounting result. Viable as secondary framing.
- **C. Innovation/intangible measurement** — support: weakest (β is residual-identified, not measured; only 7/39 significant). Rejected.

**Positioning: A (measurement-first), with B's substantive findings (TFP +1.7 pp, labour-share +1.7 pp) carried inside.**

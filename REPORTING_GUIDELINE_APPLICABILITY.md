# Reporting-guideline applicability audit

Study type: macroeconomic measurement / cross-country quantitative
analysis using public aggregate data (PWT 10.01, CWON, WDI, OECD GFCF).
Not a systematic review, not an observational epidemiological study,
not a trial, not a health-economic evaluation, not a prediction-model
study, not animal research.

- PRISMA 2020 — not applicable: this study is not a systematic review
  or meta-analysis.
- STROBE / RECORD — not applicable: no individual-level health data.
- CONSORT — not applicable: no trial.
- CHEERS — not applicable: no health economic evaluation.
- TRIPOD — not applicable: no clinical prediction model.
- ARRIVE — not applicable: no animal research.

SCED / Elsevier Guide for Authors: no reporting checklist is required
for this study type. Reproducibility is instead documented by the
one-command `make` pipeline and the archived frozen data extracts.

Decision: no reporting-guideline checklist is included in the
submission package.
